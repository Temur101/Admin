# Hyperframes Composition Brief: QuickBook (Variation 1: Problem vs Solution)

## Objective
Create a short, polished launch video showcasing how QuickBook eliminates booking chaos for barbershops and service businesses.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output-v1-problem-solution/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output-v1-problem-solution/brag.mp4`
- Format: vertical — 1080x1920 (9:16)
- Duration: 20 seconds (600 frames at 30 fps)

## Source Material
- Product: QuickBook (Telegram SaaS Appointment Platform)
- Key UI moments:
  - Chaotic chat bubbles: "А когда свободно?", "Перенесите меня"
  - Real Telegram phone mock with bot `@QuickCut_Barbershop_Bot`
  - Inline buttons: `[✂️ Мужская стрижка - 80 000 UZS]`, `[💈 Жасур]`, `[15:30]`
  - Emerald confirmation card: `✅ Запись подтверждена! 30 октября, 15:30`
  - Client self-service menu: `📅 Мои записи` with 1-tap reschedule `[🔄 Перенести на 16:30]`

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Modern, high-trust SaaS showcase
- Angle: Stop losing clients to missed calls and messaging chaos while cutting hair.
- Hook: Floating chaotic message bubbles vibrating with incoming notifications.
- Outro: "Ваше расписание заполнено. Ваши руки свободны. QuickBook."

## Visual Identity
- Background: `#0b0f19` (obsidian dark)
- Surface/Card: `#161f30` with border `#24324a`
- Primary Accent: `#3b82f6` (Telegram blue)
- Success Accent: `#10b981` (emerald green)
- Text Primary: `#f8fafc`
- Text Secondary: `#94a3b8`

## Audio
- Audio role: warm modern electronic business groove
- Music: `assets/music/music.mp3`
- SFX: interface taps, notification chime, success pop

## Storyboard
1. Scene 1 (0.0s – 3.5s): The Chaos — vibrating message bubbles asking for slots.
2. Scene 2 (3.5s – 8.0s): Telegram Bot reveal — service selection `[✂️ Мужская стрижка]`.
3. Scene 3 (8.0s – 13.0s): Master & Slot picking — `[💈 Жасур]`, `[15:30]` $\rightarrow$ `✅ Запись подтверждена`.
4. Scene 4 (13.0s – 17.0s): Self-Service & Reminders — `📅 Мои записи` $\rightarrow$ `Перенести`.
5. Scene 5 (17.0s – 20.0s): Outro — Full calendar + QuickBook branding.
